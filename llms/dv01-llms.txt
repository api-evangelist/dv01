# dv01

> dv01 is an "InvestorTech" data and analytics platform for the structured finance and consumer-lending markets, delivering standardized, validated, loan-level data and reporting to issuers, lenders, and investors. dv01 is part of Fitch Group. It publishes no public developer API; data is delivered into client environments via Snowflake Data Share, BigQuery, SFTP, and S3.
